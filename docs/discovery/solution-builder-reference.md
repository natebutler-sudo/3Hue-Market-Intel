Reviewed: September 9, 2026. Source: https://builder.3hue.net/

## Direction already agreed

3HUE Market Intel will support marketing for the markets 3HUE serves. Foundational ICP, positioning, and service-priority information should be supplied first so everyday users have a focused interface with few controls. The existing Solution Builder is the look-and-feel reference. The project remains in discovery.

## Patterns to carry forward

| Reference pattern | Intended application |
| --- | --- |
| Navy header, cyan active pills, orange accents | Maintain recognizable 3HUE visual identity. |
| Compact top navigation and search | Make the main intelligence views easy to reach; final view names remain undecided. |
| Rounded summary cards with clear hierarchy | Let marketers scan findings quickly before opening details. |
| White curated cards and darker catalog cards | Reference for differentiating content importance or purpose; the intelligence categories remain undecided. |
| Pill-shaped filters and contextual tags | Provide a limited set of relevant filters informed by the ICP. |
| Right-side detail drawer | Reveal deeper context without requiring a separate page for every finding. |
| Source links and contextual comparisons | Connect claims to evidence and distinguish observations from interpretation. |

## Observed market-comparison content

The Initial Risk Assessment drawer contains sections for regulatory applicability, standard industries, related services, market comparison, and Why 3HUE. Comparison entries include named providers or market guides, scope descriptions, indicative pricing, and links to source pages. A live AI price-check button is present. The button was not run, and neither the freshness nor accuracy of the linked pricing was validated.

For Market Intel, this suggests an evidence-oriented detail flow: what changed, which buyers care, relevant 3HUE offerings, and a possible marketing response. Sources and timestamps should support trust. This is a proposed content approach pending the foundational strategy materials, not a finalized feature specification.

## Simplification and boundaries

- Do not replicate client/contact entry, quantity inputs, discounts, quote generation, legal documents, or scheduling merely because they exist in the reference.
- Keep foundational strategy separate from everyday intelligence browsing, consistent with the user's preference to provide that information up front.
- A collection rail, saved briefs, and contextual help remain candidate workflows until marketing deliverables are defined.
- Real-time refresh, targeted research, source selection, and freshness indicators require further planning; no operational feeds exist yet.
- Preserve the visual language while making the future interface responsive and readable. Screenshot clipping and crowded layouts are not design requirements.
