## Evidence limits

- The authenticated Builder was read through its live UI; the public unauthenticated HTML endpoint does not expose this catalog.
- Prices are recorded exactly as observed, including `FROM`, monthly, annual, hourly, per-node, and fixed-fee units.
- Drawer-level inclusions, tiers, related services, regulatory applicability, industries, and competitor context were captured where exposed during the exploration pass. The representative drawer evidence is described in `catalog.md` and `triggers-and-rules.md`.
- Buyer triggers and channel guidance are separated from Builder quote drivers. A trigger marked as a hypothesis requires later validation before it becomes an automated recommendation.
