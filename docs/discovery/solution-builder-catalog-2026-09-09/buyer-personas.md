# Working buyer personas for Market Intel

These are the three working segments from the supplied ICP research. They are strategy inputs, not a validated predictive scoring model.

## Hue 01 — Provable Vendor

- **Fit:** US B2B software or tech-enabled services, roughly 50–500 employees and `$10M–$150M` revenue, selling to enterprise, healthcare, financial-services, or public-sector buyers; often SaaS-heavy or multi-cloud.
- **Buying group:** CEO/COO/CFO as economic buyer; CTO/VP Engineering or Head of Sales/RevOps as champion; counsel, insurer, board, and investor as influencers.
- **Triggers:** Enterprise deal or renewal blocked by SOC 2, ISO 27001, or a questionnaire; cyber-insurance renewal; diligence; an enterprise customer asking about AI risk.
- **Psychology:** They need proof that can move a deal. They are skeptical of a certificate-only promise and worry about cost, time, and being exposed as unprepared.
- **Where to reach them:** LinkedIn, search around customer-assurance and readiness language, SaaS/product communities, founder and revenue-leader content, and account-level job or funding signals. Channel confidence should remain labeled until measured.
- **Offer alignment:** SOC 2 readiness, risk assessment, ISG foundations, AI Snapshot, AI governance, staff augmentation, and managed programs.

## Hue 02 — Portfolio

- **Fit:** Private-equity sponsors and family offices below `$25B` AUM with roughly 5–30 lower-middle-market operating companies.
- **Buying group:** Operating partner or portfolio CFO, with investment committee, lender, board, and portfolio-company leaders as influencers or execution owners.
- **Triggers:** New platform acquisition, exit process, portfolio-company incident, lender or insurance requirement, or the need for common reporting across entities.
- **Psychology:** They want leverage across the portfolio, visibility without adding headcount, and evidence that reduces diligence and valuation risk.
- **Where to reach them:** Operating-partner networks, private-equity and family-office content, portfolio-company diligence conversations, LinkedIn, and account monitoring around acquisitions, exits, leadership changes, and incidents. These channel hypotheses need validation.
- **Offer alignment:** Portfolio-wide ISG programs, VCP, risk and incident-response programs, board/investor reporting, ITG governance, managed programs, and embedded specialists.

## Hue 03 — Regulated Operator

- **Fit:** Community and regional banks, credit unions, lenders, fintechs, and OT-heavy transportation, energy, or utility operators, generally around 200–2,000 employees.
- **Buying group:** CIO, CISO, Chief Risk Officer, compliance leadership, legal, and operational owners.
- **Triggers:** Examiner request, MRA, audit finding, remediation deadline, incident, continuity concern, or AI adoption crossing regulatory and operational boundaries.
- **Psychology:** They need defensible decisions in the examiner’s language, clear ownership, and evidence that survives scrutiny. “Fastest certificate” is not the primary motivation.
- **Where to reach them:** Regulatory and industry associations, risk/compliance communities, LinkedIn, search around examiner and framework language, and sector-specific events or publications. Treat channel performance as a measurement question.
- **Offer alignment:** ISG governance, risk, privacy, CIRP, resilience, MDR/MXDR, ITG, AI governance, and senior advisory or staff augmentation.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer buying intent from the absence of a public title, trust page, or social post.
- Keep firmographic fit, trigger evidence, channel evidence, and recommended action as separate fields.
- Preserve source date, evidence type, confidence, and whether a statement is reported fact, company claim, estimate, or hypothesis.
