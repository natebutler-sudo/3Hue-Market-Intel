## Coverage and limits

40 supplied files reviewed: text and tabular extraction covered every PDF page, HTML document, Markdown/text file, DOCX body paragraph/table, and CSV row. Alternate PDF/HTML versions were compared after normalizing text; no binary duplicate was discarded. The brand cover and complete GA4 snapshot were also visually inspected. Embedded HTML scripts were treated as source content and were not executed; the supplied interactive fit scorer was not rebuilt or functionally tested. DOCX layout and every PDF page were not subjected to a typography audit.

The source library is approximately 5 MB. It contains neither all files referenced by its historical index nor evidence that the previously used external accounts are connected to this task. That distinction is preserved in the readiness assessment.
