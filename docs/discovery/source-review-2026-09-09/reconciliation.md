## How to use conflicting versions

Choose authority by topic and explicit correction, not simply by filename, export format, or latest date. A consolidated report can preserve older guidance. A PDF and HTML rendering of the same research are one evidence lineage, not independent corroboration.

## Channel and measurement corrections

The following findings concern the supplied [Data Book](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/05-youtube/3HUE-YouTube-Data-Book.html), [keyword research](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/05-youtube/05-youtube-keyword-competitor-data.html), and [latest rollout](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/05-youtube/youtube-rollout.pdf).

| Finding | Consequence for future intelligence |
| --- | --- |
| Some rows report **<750** and guidance says to treat that as zero. | Below a reporting floor means censored or unavailable at that resolution; it does not mean zero demand. |
| The Data Book gives the seed **vciso** 3,456 monthly searches, while rollout guidance groups it with variants below 750. | Keep the seed and phrase variants separate; resolve the source rows before making a demand conclusion. |
| Tool-returned volumes are sometimes described as not estimated. | They are third-party estimates, not a census of searches or first-party company analytics. Preserve tool, geography, date, and methodology. |
| Title scores are predicted click-potential scores on a 0–100 scale. | They are not measured CTR and should not be displayed as percentages of viewers who clicked. |
| A score of 84 is called the lowest/worst although the table also contains 82 and 78. | Drop the categorical claim that specificity wins every comparison. These scores can inform hypotheses, not prove audience response. |
| An AI-regulation row has global volume 3,306 and US volume 20,250. | These cannot be compared as compatible nested geographic counts without clarifying scope and definitions. |
| Growth baselines differ or are unclear across the Data Book, rollout, and consolidation. | Do not normalize them into one time series. The September 2–9 comparison is a short interval, not a durable trend. |
| Repeated identical growth percentages appear across terms. | Possible modeling or banding is a hypothesis, not a verified explanation. Raw responses and tool definitions are needed. |
| Channel/video comparisons mix topics, ages, audiences, and possible distribution differences. | Views alone do not establish buyer quality, pipeline, causal format superiority, or competitor conversion rates. |

## Corporate claims that need dates and provenance

Some inherited audits describe missing trust/framework pages or limited visible AiVRIC connections. The [independent September 9 research](../../research/2026-09-09/README.md) records newer public evidence, and the supplied GA4 snapshot itself lists Trust & Compliance Center and framework-page views. Preserve historical observations with their dates; do not carry those absence claims forward as current facts.

Similarly, missing schema or sitemap references do not establish the cause of weak organic performance. Historical 2024 SEO numbers do not prove continuous ranking failure through 2026. Website repairs remain a separate workstream.

The messaging library's “substantiated” label often points to company-controlled material. That may establish what 3HUE claims, but does not equal independently audited proof or approval for every new publication. Competitive statements about MSPs, solo practitioners, tools, and doing nothing should be treated as positioning hypotheses unless supported by specific comparisons or win/loss records. Sources: [Message Stack](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/03-messaging-and-campaigns/message-stack.pdf), [Ship List](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/03-messaging-and-campaigns/ship-list.html).

Proposed teardown capacity, turnaround times, Snapshot outputs, and readiness letters should not silently become universal service promises. Regulatory dates and legal interpretations in inherited research also require current primary-source verification before they drive public claims or deadline alerts. This review did not refresh every external citation.

## Analytics: what the supplied files establish

The [GA4 report](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/06-analytics/google-analytics/Reports_snapshot.pdf) covers August 11–September 7, 2026. It shows 443 active users, 440 new users, seven seconds average engagement per active user, and 1.7K events. Google organic contributes 14 first-user acquisition users and 29 sessions; these are different measures. The key-events panel has no data available. That is insufficient to conclude there were zero leads or that no tracking exists.

The [Search Console files](../../../assets/strategy/2026-09-08-09-source-library/06-analytics/google-search-console) contain 92 daily rows from June 7–September 6, all with zero clicks/impressions and blank CTR/position. Countries, Devices, Pages, Queries, and Search appearance are header-only exports. Filters identifies Web and Last 3 months, but no property identifier.

The GA4/GSC discrepancy needs property scope, date/filter, and measurement checks. It does not establish zero organic audience. City names and short engagement alone cannot reliably identify bots or disqualify visitors. These snapshots are a partial historical baseline, not a live integration, attribution model, or CRM funnel.

## Implications for planning

The supplied ICP and fit model are sufficient as provisional business context. Their 60/25/15 emphasis and scoring weights still need validation against actual outcomes before the product claims predictive prioritization. Insurance renewals, internal exam findings, and staffing levels may not be publicly observable; absence of a title or trust page is not confirmation of a buying trigger. Future planning should preserve source dates, evidence type, confidence, and explicit supersession. It should separate reported facts, company claims, estimates, hypotheses, and proposed actions. References in these historical files to delegated work, approvals, account access, or launching campaigns are contextual records; they do not activate new work. The current instruction remains discovery only.
