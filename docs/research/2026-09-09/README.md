Research date: **September 9, 2026**. Status: **discovery only**. No website, integration, automated monitor, or campaign was built or launched.

This dossier establishes a public-evidence baseline before the owner supplies an ICP or internal strategy. Three research agents investigated separate areas, with a fourth editorial workstream and consolidation by the lead agent. Research used company pages, public corporate records, official partner directories, competitors' own publications, government statistics, regulators, and standards bodies. Each report contains source links and limitations.

## Executive interpretation

**The most promising intelligence focus is the intersection of AI adoption, governance, security, and enterprise assurance.** This is our synthesis of the public portfolio, not a confirmed revenue priority. A useful marketing team brief would connect a specific external change to an affected buyer, a plausible business problem, a relevant offering, and an evidence-backed next action. The detailed service mapping is in [report 01](01-company-and-services.md); buyer and demand analysis is in [report 02](02-markets-buyers-and-demand.md).

**Market coverage needs a deliberate decision.** Public evidence supports researching financial services, SaaS/digital products, energy/OT, private equity/family offices, and transportation. It does not establish their relative profitability or priority. The transportation URL currently displays energy content, while the industry index still describes transportation. This makes an inferred, fixed ICP premature. [Industry evidence and exact sources](02-markets-buyers-and-demand.md)

**The competitive set must reflect different buying choices.** Advisory firms, enterprise consultancies, assurance providers, and software platforms overlap in buyer problems but differ in responsibilities. The provisional seven-company comparison is a research starting point; it is not a ranking or a verified win/loss list. [Competitive evidence](03-competitive-landscape-and-public-proof.md)

**Proof quality is a material marketing consideration.** The research establishes an external business footprint and records company-published customer outcomes. It does not establish independently verified outcome rates, provider certifications, or proprietary performance advantages. AiVRIC has documented operational ties to 3HUE, while precise legal ownership and deployed capability verification remain open. [Proof and relationship analysis](03-competitive-landscape-and-public-proof.md)

**Freshness should be part of the intelligence discipline.** Existing pages contain expired availability copy and inconsistent offer durations and article metadata. Future recommendations should preserve event dates, source dates, and retrieval dates, and identify when supporting evidence changes. This is a research recommendation, not an approved implementation requirement. [Content findings](04-marketing-content-and-intelligence-opportunities.md)

## Decisions this research can inform

The following sequence is recommended after review; it does not require supplying documents to consider the findings now.

1. Choose the first commercial objective: introductory AI advisory, expansion of managed programs, or another explicitly prioritized offer.
2. Resolve the primary sector list and distinguish it from adjacent research coverage.
3. Confirm who marketers need to influence: executive sponsors, security/technology leaders, assurance owners, portfolio buyers, or a combination.
4. Select initial marketing outputs, such as sector briefs, account research, content opportunities, competitor updates, and sales-enablement evidence.
5. Establish which claims and customer references are approved and what evidence supports each.
6. Define source freshness expectations by information type. Event-driven news, periodic statistics, and occasional standards updates have different useful cadences.

## Evidence classification

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified public record | A primary authority supports the specific recorded fact; this does not validate unrelated claims |
| Company-published claim | Evidence of the company's public positioning, offering, or reported outcome |
| External corroboration | Another publisher supports a bounded fact; a directory listing is weaker than a customer statement or independent examination |
| Analyst inference | Interpretation, suggested relevance, or proposed action derived from cited evidence |
| Unvalidated / unknown | Public research did not establish the answer; no estimate is substituted |

All reports are dated observations rather than exhaustive audits. Source pages and search extracts may change or lag; publication dates were not invented from search-engine crawl dates. A failed lookup or unsuccessful search is not proof that something does not exist. Original source documents are linked rather than reproduced in full.

## Important unresolved inputs

- Recent buying committees, sales-cycle evidence, competitors encountered, and win/loss reasons.
- Approved customer references, outcome evidence, partner status, and own-provider assurance artifacts.
- Analytics, traffic, attribution, campaign performance, and paid-media economics.

These are the boundaries of independent public research, not missing invented numbers. The eventual owner-provided materials can be stored in `assets/strategy/` and reconciled against this dated baseline while preserving the original findings.
