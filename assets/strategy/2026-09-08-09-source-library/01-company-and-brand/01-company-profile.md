### What the name means (founder's account, call of 2026-09-03 — full reconstruction in note 07)
- **Origin:** three founders of three different hues; Andrew calls it "an inclusive name." Named at a cafe on day five of a Las Vegas trip whose only purpose was to see whether the three got along as people before doing business.
- **Form:** back in Florida, needed a final name for the articles of incorporation; set "3 HUE" in caps and closed the space → 3HUE. "3HUE Consulting" sounded off ("we don't consult for paint") → what do we consult for? Executives → 3HUE Executive Consulting.
- **Retired reading:** "the three hues of security" — a marketing line he tried and dropped.
- **Current meaning:** a standard, not an acronym. "The highest possible level of execution." Used as an adjective: "get a 3HUE on it," like special forces / tiger team. The reason he guards the brand: a deliverable "can't look like a document somebody else made."
- **The three:** a pattern, not a plan — every business he has started carries a three (Trilogy Multimedia Enterprises); Trinity; "all great things come in threes"; Tesla 3-6-9. ISG/OPS/ITG are one downstream application, not the referent.
- **Sensitivity:** the origin is personal and public only in his own telling. The "standard" meaning is the public one.
