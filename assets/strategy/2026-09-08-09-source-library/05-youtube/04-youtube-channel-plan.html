The two decisive format findings

1. ControlCase is a controlled experiment: webinars lose by 3 orders of magnitude.

Short plain-English explainers: - Phishing Emails (8:18) — 2,960,874 - Password Managers (9:20) — 212,387 - Multi-Factor Auth (9:07) — 182,563 - Strong Passwords (7:01) — 177,113

Recorded webinars: - SOC 2 Compliance (28:16) — 1,641 - What is ISO 27001? (27:04) — 1,042 - Demystifying CMMC (1:09:15) — 671 - ISO 27001 for MSPs (25:32) — 647

Caveat: the big numbers are consumer-grade security topics that likely convert nothing. Take the format lesson, not the topic lesson.

2. Stuart Barker's outliers = contrarian + product + price. - "Why you don't need a GRC Platform for ISO 27001" (2:21) — 102,401 (biggest organic) - "ISO 27001 Toolkit Demo and Product Overview" (42:44) — 77,593 - "ISO 27001 Getting Started" (43:34) — 35,330 - "ISO 27001 Certification Cost Explained Simply" (14:13) — 30,928 - "ISO 27001 vs ISO 27002 Explained Simply" (5:57) — 13,711 - Clause-by-clause library: 2,000–10,000 each

Library average ≈ 1,000 views/video and it sustains a real business. Set expectations accordingly.

3. Shorts are the growth engine in this niche. Dejan 527/yr → 426K subs; Luv Johar shorts 2.55M views/yr; Stuart ~61/month.

The slate (six series)

Series Role Length Cadence
Explained Simply Reach — search capture 5–8 min 2/wk
The Control Room Reach — reference library, SEO moat 8–15 min 1/wk
CEO's Corner (Nate × Andrew) Trust — POV, clip factory 12–18 min 2/mo
Teardown (anonymised cases) Proof — converts 15–25 min 1/mo
How We Work (processes) Proof — sales collateral 6–10 min fixed 6-part
The Stack (AiVRIC / tooling) Proof — feeds free tier 8–20 min 2/mo
Shorts distribution layer, cut from the above 30–60s 5/wk

CEO's Corner is deliberately not the flagship — nobody in this niche wins with founder-interview long-form, and ControlCase's own talking-head webinars are their worst performers. It earns its slot as the contrarian/POV band and as the clip factory for LinkedIn.

Phase 1 (months 1–3) = Explained Simply 8/mo + CEO's Corner 2/mo + the 6 How We Work videos + 12 shorts/mo. Achievable in two filming days a month.

Two open decisions for Nate

  1. Channel identity — recommendation: 3HUE as the channel, Andrew as face/voice, AiVRIC named only inside The Stack. Clips distribute from Andrew's personal LinkedIn.
  2. Will 3HUE publish what things cost? "What SOC 2 actually costs" is a top-five performer for every consultant who has made it, and 3hue.net publishes no pricing. Engagement bands would suffice. If no, cut the video rather than making a vague one.

Measurement (first 90 days)

  1. Search impressions on the SOC 2 cluster (YouTube Studio traffic sources) — the only phase-1 metric that matters
  2. LinkedIn clip engagement vs. Andrew's article baseline (17–27 reactions)
  3. "I saw your video" on sales calls — ask every call, write it down
